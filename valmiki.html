राTHE RAMAYANA Back to the story

Source guide · Valmiki Ramayana

Six books, one unfolding journey

These concise summaries are original descriptions of the story arc reflected in the six Kanda indexes available at ValmikiRamayan.net. Follow the links to explore the source site’s English translation and chapter-by-chapter contents. This guide follows those six indexes and does not cover Uttara Kanda.

Bala Kanda

Rama’s early life leads from Ayodhya to the court of Mithila, where he meets Sita and their marriage joins the two royal families.

Explore Bala Kanda chapters

Ayodhya Kanda

A change in the succession sends Rama into forest exile with Sita and Lakshmana. Bharata refuses to rule in Rama’s place and safeguards the kingdom during his absence.

Explore Ayodhya Kanda chapters

Aranya Kanda

In the forest, encounters with its inhabitants culminate in Sita’s abduction. Rama’s search brings grief, new allies, and a path toward the southern coast.

Explore Aranya Kanda chapters

Kishkindha Kanda

Rama and Sugriva form an alliance. Search parties spread out to find Sita, and Hanuman emerges as the one able to cross the ocean toward Lanka.

Explore Kishkindha Kanda chapters

Sundara Kanda

Hanuman reaches Lanka, finds Sita in the Ashoka garden, and carries news and a token between her and Rama before returning to the search party.

Explore Sundara Kanda chapters

Yuddha Kanda

Rama’s allies prepare an ocean crossing and confront Ravana’s forces. After the conflict in Lanka, the story follows the return to Ayodhya and Rama’s coronation.

Explore Yuddha Kanda chapters
